# Research Methods Reference 研究方法參考資源

The methodological backbone behind [Lesson 06 — From Data to Spatial Insight](../../lessons/06-spatial-insight/).
[課程 06 — 從資料到空間洞見](../../lessons/06-spatial-insight/)背後的方法論基礎。

## The What/Where/Why/So-what chain 四問鏈

The core interpretation method taught in this workshop:
本工作坊教授的核心詮釋方法：

1. **What?** Describe the pattern in the data, without interpreting yet. 描述資料中的樣式，尚不詮釋。
2. **Where?** Locate where the pattern concentrates or varies. 定位樣式集中或變化之處。
3. **Why?** Propose a grounded hypothesis for the pattern. 提出有根據的假設。
4. **So what?** State the implication for the research question, design, or policy. 陳述其對研究問題、設計或政策的意涵。

A map answers *What* and *Where* directly. *Why* and *So what* require your own reasoning — the map alone cannot do this for you.
地圖能直接回答「是什麼」與「在哪裡」，但「為什麼」與「所以呢」需要你自己的推理——地圖本身無法代勞。

## Formulating a good spatial research question 提出好的空間研究問題

A strong spatial question usually: 好的空間問題通常：

- Names a specific place or comparable set of places 指出特定地點或可比較的一組地點
- Implies at least one measurable spatial variable 隱含至少一個可測量的空間變數
- Can be answered partially by "where," fully only with "why"/"so what" 部分可由「在哪裡」回答，需要「為什麼」／「所以呢」才能完整回答

Weak: "Tell me about this neighborhood." 弱：「介紹一下這個社區。」
Strong: "Where are the greener public spaces, and does that correlate with building age?" 強：「哪裡是較綠意盎然的公共空間，這是否與建築年齡有關？」

## Evidence, not conclusion 證據，而非結論

Treat every map, chart, and statistic as *one piece of evidence* supporting a written argument. State your argument in prose; use the map to show it, not to replace it.
把每張地圖、圖表與統計數字都視為支持書面論證的**一項證據**。用文字陳述你的論點，用地圖來呈現，而非取代論點。
