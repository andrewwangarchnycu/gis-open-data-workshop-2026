# Figure-Ground 圖底關係

Borrowed directly from architectural drawing convention — most famously the Nolli map of Rome, where solid buildings (ground) and voids/public space (figure) are rendered in deliberate black-and-white contrast.
直接借用自建築製圖傳統——最著名的例子是羅馬 Nolli 地圖，將實體建築（底）與虛體／公共空間（圖）以刻意的黑白對比呈現。

## Core question 核心問題

**What is figure (the subject of analysis) and what is ground (context)?**
**什麼是圖（分析主體）？什麼是底（脈絡背景）？**

This is a design decision, not an automatic fact about the data. The same buildings layer can be figure (a study of building form) or ground (context for a study of public space) depending on your question.
這是一個設計決策，並非資料本身自動決定的事實。同一組建築圖層，依你的問題不同，可以是圖（建築量體研究）也可以是底（公共空間研究的脈絡背景）。

## Applying it to a research map 應用於研究地圖

1. Identify your analysis subject — this is figure. Render it with the highest contrast/saturation. 找出你的分析主體——即為圖。以最高對比／飽和度呈現。
2. Everything else — roads, unrelated buildings, base context — is ground. Render it muted: grey, low-contrast, low-saturation. 其餘一切——道路、無關建築、底圖脈絡——皆為底，以低對比、低飽和度（灰階）呈現。
3. Avoid rendering figure and ground with similar visual weight — the whole point is that figure should visually separate immediately. 避免圖與底的視覺份量相近——重點正是圖應能立即與底區隔開來。

## Example 範例

Researching tree density in public spaces: 研究公共空間內的樹木密度：

- **Figure 圖**: public space polygons, colored by tree density (the subject) 公共空間面，依樹木密度上色（研究主體）
- **Ground 底**: building footprints and roads, in flat light grey (context only) 建築足跡與道路，以平面淺灰呈現（僅作脈絡）
