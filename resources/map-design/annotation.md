# Annotation 標註

**Purpose 目的**: highlight the important spatial finding directly on the map — don't make the viewer hunt for it in a caption.
直接在地圖上標出重要的空間發現，不要讓觀者在圖說文字中自行尋找。

## Why annotation matters 為什麼標註很重要

A map can show a pattern without telling anyone which part of it is the point. Annotation closes that gap — it's the difference between "here is data" and "here is what I found."
地圖可以呈現樣式，卻不會主動告訴觀者重點在哪。標註能補上這個落差——這是「這是資料」與「這是我的發現」之間的差別。

## Techniques 技巧

- **Callout line + short label** — a thin line from the location to a one-line caption. 從位置拉出一條細線，連接一句話的標籤。
- **Circle or box highlight** — a light outline around the area of interest, used sparingly. 於關注區域畫上淡色外框，需節制使用。
- **Direct labeling** — placing the label right next to the feature instead of relying on a legend to decode it. 直接將標籤放在要素旁，而非仰賴圖例解讀。

## Writing the annotation text 撰寫標註文字

One sentence, specific, tied to the insight — not a restatement of the legend.
一句話，具體，緊扣洞見——而非重複圖例內容。

> Weak 弱: "High density area" 「高密度區域」
> Strong 強: "Historic core: lowest tree coverage (15%)" 「歷史核心區：樹木覆蓋率最低（15%）」

## How many annotations 標註數量

1–3 per map. More than that competes with the main pattern and undermines [visual hierarchy](visual-hierarchy.md). If you have five things to point out, you likely need five maps, or a clearer single question.
每張地圖 1–3 個。過多標註會與主要樣式互相競爭，破壞[視覺層級](visual-hierarchy.md)。若有五個重點要標示，可能代表你需要五張地圖，或應更聚焦於單一問題。
