# 01 — Mapping the Unknown 探索未知空間
*10 minutes 分鐘*

## What is GIS? 什麼是 GIS？

GIS (Geographic Information System) is a system for capturing, storing, analyzing, and communicating data that has a **location**. But the software is not the point — the point is what location-aware data lets you *ask and answer*.

GIS（地理資訊系統）是一套用來擷取、儲存、分析並傳達具有**位置**的資料的系統。但軟體本身不是重點，重點是具有位置資訊的資料能讓你**提出並回答**什麼問題。

> **GIS ≠ map making.**
> **GIS 不等於製圖。**

Making a map is often the *output*. GIS as a discipline is about the *reasoning* that gets you there: what varies across space, why, and what that means.
製圖經常只是**產出**。GIS 作為一門學科，關注的是抵達產出之前的**推理過程**：什麼東西在空間中有所變化、為什麼變化，以及這代表什麼意義。

## What is spatial thinking? 什麼是空間思考？

Spatial thinking is reasoning about:
空間思考是針對以下面向進行推理：

- **Location** — where something is 位置——某事物在哪裡
- **Distribution** — how it's spread out 分布——如何散布
- **Pattern** — whether it clusters, spreads evenly, or is random 樣式——聚集、均勻分布或隨機
- **Relationship** — how it relates to other things nearby 關係——與鄰近事物的關聯

Architects already do this intuitively when they read a site: where is the sun, where do people walk, where does the noise come from. GIS formalizes that intuition into data you can measure, compare, and communicate.
建築人在解讀基地時早已直覺地做這件事：陽光在哪、人行走的路徑在哪、噪音來自哪裡。GIS 把這種直覺形式化為可測量、可比較、可傳達的資料。

## GIS as a research method 作為研究方法的 GIS

Think of GIS the way you'd think of a literature review or a site survey — a method for building evidence, not a deliverable in itself.
把 GIS 想成如同文獻回顧或基地調查一般——是建立證據的方法，而非本身即是最終產出。

```text
Research Question 研究問題
↓
Open Geospatial Data 開放地理資料
↓
GIS Exploration GIS 探索
↓
Computational Spatial Analysis 運算式空間分析
↓
Spatial Insight 空間洞見
↓
Research Map 研究地圖
```

Every module in this workshop is one link in that chain. By the end, you will have walked the whole chain once, from a question of your own to a finished research map.
本工作坊的每個模組都是這條鏈上的一環。到結束時，你將從自己的問題出發，完整走過一次這條鏈，並產出一張完成的研究地圖。
