# Research Map Examples 研究地圖範例

Reference maps demonstrating the design principles from [Lesson 07](../../lessons/07-research-map-design/) and the [checklist](../../resources/map-design/research-map-checklist.md). Use these for critique practice, not for copying layouts verbatim.
展示[課程 07](../../lessons/07-research-map-design/)與[檢查清單](../../resources/map-design/research-map-checklist.md)設計原則的參考地圖。用於評圖練習，而非直接複製版面。

## What makes a good example here 好範例的條件

- States a clear research question as the title. 以清楚的研究問題作為標題。
- Uses color to encode a variable, not to decorate. 用色彩傳達變數，而非裝飾。
- Includes an annotation pointing at the key finding. 包含指向關鍵發現的標註。
- States one insight in a sentence, not just a legend. 以一句話陳述洞見，而非僅有圖例。
